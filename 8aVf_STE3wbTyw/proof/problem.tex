\section*{Problem Statement}

\textbf{Proposition (AI is the Future):} Artificial Intelligence (AI) represents
the defining technological paradigm of humanity's future development.

\medskip

More precisely, we establish the following four pillars:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Technological Inevitability:} The trajectory of computational
    progress---governed by empirical scaling laws and sustained hardware
    advances---implies AI systems will continue improving at an accelerating
    rate, making regression to a pre-AI paradigm effectively impossible.

  \item \textbf{Economic Transformation:} AI-driven automation and optimization
    restructure labor markets, productivity, and economic growth in ways that
    make AI adoption economically irreversible at a societal scale.

  \item \textbf{Scientific Acceleration:} AI augments human cognitive capacity
    in research and discovery, acting as a force multiplier across all scientific
    disciplines and shortening the timeline from question to breakthrough.

  \item \textbf{Societal Integration:} The pervasive embedding of AI into
    critical infrastructure, healthcare, education, and governance makes its
    role as a foundational future technology structurally unavoidable.
\end{enumerate}

\medskip
\textbf{Goal:} Provide a rigorous, multi-faceted argument establishing each
of the four pillars above and synthesize them into a coherent proof of the
proposition.
