ISSUE 1 — CRITICAL ERROR
Location: Theorem conclusion — "Then for every d >= 1 and every rho > 0,
  reverting has strictly negative NPV relative to continuing AI. Hence,
  within this two-strategy model, continuing AI is the unique NPV-
  maximizing strategy."
Description:
  The corrected theorem's listed assumptions do not include Pi_0 >= 0,
  yet the claimed NPV dominance can fail when Pi_0 < 0. Under A2 one
  may have Pi_AI(d) = (1 + gamma) * Pi_0, so the productivity-stream
  term (Pi_0 - Pi_AI(d)) / rho = -gamma * Pi_0 / rho is strictly
  positive whenever Pi_0 < 0, and can outweigh the switching cost c*d^2.
  The theorem as stated is therefore not valid from its declared
  assumptions.

ISSUE 2 — JUSTIFICATION GAP
Location: Algebraic step — "Since c > 0, d >= 1, gamma > 0, rho > 0,
  and Pi_0 >= 0, we have  -c*d^2 - gamma*Pi_0 / rho < 0."
Description:
  The proof invokes Pi_0 >= 0 as a fact, but this condition appears in
  neither A2, A4, nor any additional assumption listed in the corrected
  theorem. Its source is entirely unjustified. This is a direct
  consequence of Issue 1: the assumption is silently smuggled in to
  make the inequality hold, but it is not part of the theorem's
  hypothesis set.
