Description: Justification Gap: \(A1\) gives a scaling relation for large neural-network models trained with compute \(C\); it does not establish that this scaling-law family is the best AI system purchasable within budget \(B\), that the budget always places the system in the “large model” regime, or that no other AI system achieves a different loss. The equality between the scaling-law loss and the “best loss achievable” is therefore not justified.

Description: Justification Gap: The stated economic assumption is not enough to prove economic dominance as previously defined.
